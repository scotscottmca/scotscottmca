MEM Patching Optimizer (MEMPO), aka **Project Clippy**, is a tool I helped build
at Patch My PC. It takes the most common issues we see in customer WSUS and
ConfigMgr environments, finds them, reports on them, and points you at the fix —
a bit like a "clippy" for WSUS.

I worked on this alongside a few other Patch My PC engineers. We planned it out
week by week, and it taught me a huge amount about both development and the inner
workings of WSUS.

Use the link above to learn more about what it does and how it can help clean up
a patching environment.
